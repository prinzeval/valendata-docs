You pay for what a run uses, in credits. Every part of a run is metered on its own and priced at the rates below. There are no flat fees on top.

New accounts get **100 free credits**, no card needed. After that, credits come from a [monthly plan or a one-time top-up](#getting-credits), at **\$1 = 100 credits**.

## How a run is charged

When you run a skill or workflow, Valendata charges credits for what that run used. Charges are counted in steps of 0.01 credit and never rounded up to a whole credit. Browser time is billed per second while your run holds the browser, from when the session starts until the run finishes. Idle gaps between the steps of a running workflow count.

<Note>
Browser time after your run ends is free. Valendata may keep a finished run's browser open for a couple of minutes so your next run starts faster. You are not charged for that time.
</Note>

Every attempt is charged for what it used, whatever the outcome. Credits are not given back when a run fails, returns no rows, or stops at a human check, or when a skill creation, workflow, or repair does not work. This is true even when the failure is on our side (our proxy, our cloud browser, a worker restart, an error in our code, or an AI provider outage). See [Runs](/concepts/runs#why-a-run-failed).

### AI model usage

The cost depends on the model a run uses and how many tokens it uses:

| Model | Input (credits / 1k tokens) | Output (credits / 1k tokens) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gemini 3.7 Flash *(default)* | 0.1875 | 0.9375 |
| Gemini 3.1 Pro | 0.50 | 3.00 |
| Claude Sonnet 4.6 | 0.75 | 3.75 |
| Claude Opus 4.6 | 1.25 | 6.25 |
| Claude Opus 4.7 | 1.25 | 6.25 |
| GPT-OSS 20B *(free, for simple tasks)* | 0 | 0 |

If you bring your own key (BYOK), you pay your AI provider directly for tokens. You still pick the model, but the token cost is between you and your provider.

### Browser and proxy time

| Resource | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Browser session time | 2.0 credits / minute |
| + Sticky Identity proxy *(one stable IP that stays signed in to one site)* | +2.2 credits / minute, on top of browser time |
| + Rotating Residential proxy *(residential IPs, from the right region)* | +2.9 credits / minute, on top of browser time |

Browser time is billed per second at this rate, in steps of 0.01 credit: 45 seconds costs 1.5 credits, and 60.04 seconds costs 2.00 credits. Not every task needs a proxy. Valendata decides based on what the task needs, and a proxy surcharge applies only for the time that proxy is in use.

### Other metered actions

| Resource | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Web search query | 2.0 credits / query |
| Sandbox code execution | 1.0 credit / execution |
| Sandbox time while your code runs | 1.25 credits / minute (idle time is free) |
| Extracted data row | 0.1 credit / row (10 rows = 1 credit) |
| [Connected app](/concepts/connected-apps) call | 0.1 credit / read, 0.5 credit / write |
| Verification handling — reCAPTCHA v2 | 0.20 credits / solve |
| Verification handling — reCAPTCHA v3 | 0.25 credits / solve |
| Verification handling — Cloudflare Turnstile | 0.30 credits / solve |

Verification handling happens, and is billed, only when a task meets a challenge, and only where the site permits it. Most tasks never meet one. If a site refuses access, the run stops and reports `blocked` instead of trying to get around it.

## Getting credits

Every new account starts with **100 free credits**, no card needed. After that, credits come from a monthly plan, from one-time top-ups, or both. **\$1 = 100 credits.**

### Monthly plans

Plans are monthly subscriptions. Each one adds its credits every month on your billing date. The bigger plans cost less per credit:

| Plan | Credits a month | Price | Price per credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 1,000 | \$10 / month | \$0.0100 |
| Growth | 5,000 | \$45 / month *(10% off)* | \$0.0090 |
| Pro | 10,000 | \$80 / month *(20% off)* | \$0.0080 |

Plan credits reset to the plan's amount on each billing date. Cancel any time: the plan stops renewing, and you keep it until the end of the month you paid for.

### One-time top-ups

Top up any amount, any time, at \$1 for 100 credits. A top-up is a single payment, not a subscription. **Top-up credits never expire**, and they stay separate from your plan's monthly credits, so a monthly reset never takes them away.

To buy more than 50,000 credits a month, email [info@valendata.com](mailto:info@valendata.com).

## Checking your credit balance

Click your credit balance in the top header to open the **Credits & Billing** panel. It shows your transaction history and what each run cost, and lets you buy credits. Credits are added right after payment.

From code, read your balance with [`GET /v1/account`](/api-reference/account/get) and what you spent with [`GET /v1/usage`](/api-reference/account/usage). Every run result reports its own `credits_charged`. See [Runs](/concepts/runs#what-a-finished-run-holds).

## Who pays in a workspace

Runs in a workspace draw from the workspace owner's balance, whoever starts them. The owner can cap each member's monthly spend. See [Workspaces](/concepts/workspaces#shared-credit-balance).

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="What happens to my credits when I top up?">
  The top-up credits are added to your balance right away and never expire. Your plan's monthly credits keep resetting on your billing date as usual.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need a plan to start?">
  No. Every new account starts with 100 free credits, no card needed. Use them to create and test your first skills, then pick a monthly plan when you need more.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I cancel my plan?">
  Yes, any time. The plan stops renewing, and you keep it until the end of the month you paid for. Top-up credits stay in your balance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I buy a very large amount?">
  Yes. For more than 50,000 credits a month, email [info@valendata.com](mailto:info@valendata.com).
</Accordion>
