---
title: "Get a schedule"
description: "One schedule, skill or workflow."
openapi: "GET /v1/schedules/{schedule_id}"
---

A skill schedule also shows `delivery`, `notify_on`, `watch`, `webhook_url`, and the skill's `row_key` (`null` means automatic). See [Only what's new](/guides/schedule#only-whats-new).

Needs `skills:read` for a skill schedule (`sklsch_…`), `workflows:read` for a workflow schedule (`wfsch_…`).
