---
title: "Update a workflow webhook"
description: "Turn a workflow's webhook off or on, or require a signature."
openapi: "PATCH /v1/workflows/{slug}/webhook"
---

Send only what you want to change:

- `enabled`: `false` turns it off, and its URL answers `404` and starts nothing. `true` turns it back on.
- `require_signature`: `true` refuses any POST without a valid signature. See [Signing](/guides/start-from-a-webhook#signing).

Needs `workflows:write`, plus `workflows:invoke` to turn it on.
