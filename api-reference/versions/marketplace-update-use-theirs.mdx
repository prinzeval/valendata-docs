---
title: "Take the original's version after a clash"
description: "After an update clashed with your changes, take the marketplace version instead."
openapi: "POST /v1/skills/{slug}/marketplace-update/use-theirs"
---

Use it when [Check for a marketplace update](/api-reference/versions/marketplace-update-check) says `reason: "clash"`. The original's version goes live as a new version of your copy, and your copy counts as up to date. Your earlier version stays in the history, and you can [restore](/api-reference/versions/restore) it.

The answer is the update check as it is now. In the app, this is **Use theirs** in the skill's **Versions** tab.

Owner or workspace editor. Needs `skills:write`.
