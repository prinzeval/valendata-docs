---
title: "Take a marketplace update"
description: "Bring the marketplace's newer tested version into your copy, merged onto your own changes."
openapi: "POST /v1/skills/{slug}/marketplace-update"
---

Your copy gets the original's changes since you copied it. Changes you made yourself are kept, and `kept_yours` lists them. Inputs and output fields the update brings that your copy lacks are added (`added_inputs`, `added_outputs`). Yours are never changed. The result is saved as a new version, so you can [restore](/api-reference/versions/restore) the one before.

If you and the author changed the same step in different ways, nothing is applied. The update is saved as version `candidate_version`, unapplied. [Compare it](/api-reference/versions/diff), then [keep your version](/api-reference/versions/marketplace-update-keep-mine) or [take the original's](/api-reference/versions/marketplace-update-use-theirs).

How updates reach a copy: [Updates from the original](/guides/use-the-marketplace#updates-from-the-original). Owner or workspace editor. Needs `skills:write`. MCP tool: `update_from_marketplace` with `apply: true`.
