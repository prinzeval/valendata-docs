---
title: "Keep your version after a clash"
description: "After an update clashed with your changes, keep your own version."
openapi: "POST /v1/skills/{slug}/marketplace-update/keep-mine"
---

Use it when [Check for a marketplace update](/api-reference/versions/marketplace-update-check) says `reason: "clash"`. The update that clashed is set aside and not offered again. The original's next tested version is offered as usual.

The answer is the update check as it is now. In the app, this is **Keep mine** in the skill's **Versions** tab.

Owner or workspace editor. Needs `skills:write`.
