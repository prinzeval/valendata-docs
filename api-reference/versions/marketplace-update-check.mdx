---
title: "Check for a marketplace update"
description: "See whether the marketplace skill your copy came from has a newer tested version."
openapi: "GET /v1/skills/{slug}/marketplace-update"
---

`available` says whether the original has a newer tested version for your copy. `copied_version` is the version your copy holds, `latest_version` the marketplace's newest, and `changes` says in plain words what an offered update changes. `auto_update` says whether the copy takes each tested version by itself.

When nothing is offered, `reason` says why:

| `reason` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `not_a_copy` | The skill was not cloned from the marketplace. |
| `source_gone` | The original is no longer in the marketplace. |
| `up_to_date` | Your copy has the newest tested version. |
| `waiting_proof` | A newer version exists but has not proved itself yet. |
| `clash` | The newest version clashed with your own changes. It is saved as `candidate_version`. Choose with [Keep your version](/api-reference/versions/marketplace-update-keep-mine) or [Take the original's version](/api-reference/versions/marketplace-update-use-theirs). |

How updates reach a copy: [Updates from the original](/guides/use-the-marketplace#updates-from-the-original). Owner or workspace only. Needs `skills:read`. MCP tool: `update_from_marketplace`.
