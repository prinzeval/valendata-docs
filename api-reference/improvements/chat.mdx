---
title: "Read a skill's chat"
description: "The skill's chat, oldest first: your messages, its answers, and what happened on its own."
openapi: "GET /v1/skills/{slug}/doctor"
---

Returns the last 100 messages, oldest first. Each message has a `role`:

| Role | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `user` | A message you sent. |
| `doctor` | The skill's answer. |
| `event` | Something that happened on its own: a run failed, or a repair started or ended. |

A message can carry an `action` that links what it is about: its `kind` (`improvement`, `run_failed`, `test_run`, `settings`, `login`, or `restore`), with the `run_id` or `improvement_id` and its `status`.

`working` is `true` while the skill is looking into something or repairing itself. Its result is posted here when it is done.

Only the skill's owner or an editor in its workspace can read it. Needs `skills:read`.

To send a message, see [Talk to a skill](/api-reference/improvements/talk).
