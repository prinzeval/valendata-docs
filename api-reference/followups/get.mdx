---
title: "Get a follow-up"
description: "One follow-up: its status, and once it ended, its outcome and what was found."
openapi: "GET /v1/followups/{followup_id}"
---

Poll this after [Create a follow-up](/api-reference/followups/create). While `status` is `waiting`, `next_check_at` is when the place is looked in next and `checks` how many times it has been. Once `status` is `done`, `outcome` is `found`, `time_up`, or `expired`, and `found` holds only the thing that arrived: its `kind`, a one-line `summary`, and the `code`, `link`, `file_url` and `file_name`, or a short `text`, with `sender`, `subject`, and `received_at`. `note` is one plain line on how it ended.

`cancelled` and `failed` follow-ups stay readable; `failed` means the place could not be checked (`note` says why, for example the app was disconnected).

Only your own; anyone else gets `404`. Needs `followups:read`. MCP tool: `get_followup`.
