---
title: "Delete a workflow"
description: "Delete a workflow you own and take its schedule off."
openapi: "DELETE /v1/workflows/{slug}"
---

Its runs are kept: they stay in your run history and usage, and stay readable with [Get a run](/api-reference/runs/get) (`workflow_id` reads `null` once the workflow is gone). Deleting the workflow cannot be undone. `schedules_disabled` says whether a schedule was taken off.

Owner only. Needs `workflows:write`. MCP tool: `delete_workflow`.
