---
title: "Use the marketplace"
description: "Find a ready-made skill, clone it into your workspace, rate it, or share your own."
---

The **marketplace** is a public library of skills. Instead of building a skill from scratch, you can browse the marketplace, find one that matches your use case, and clone it into your workspace in a single click. The cloned skill is yours to run and customize, changes you make do not affect the original, and updates to the original are not pushed to your copy automatically.

## Browse

Open **Marketplace** from the main sidebar. It has two tabs:

- **Skills** — single skills you can clone, then run
- **Workflows** — workflows shared by the community

The **Skills** tab lists public skills in a searchable grid, sorted by Trending, Most Runs, or Recent. Each card shows the skill name, description, and creator. Click a card to open the full skill detail page, which shows Total Runs, total Clones, Rating, and credits per run before you commit.

<Tip>
  Use the search bar to filter by keyword — try the site name, data type, or use case you have in mind.
</Tip>

From code or an AI assistant, find a skill by its task with [Find a skill for a task](/api-reference/marketplace/search) or the `search_skills` [MCP tool](/ai-assistants/mcp-tools#marketplace). Find a workflow the same way with `kind=workflow` or the `search_workflows` tool.

Anyone may read a public skill or workflow. You run only your own copy. Clone it first with [Clone a skill](/api-reference/skills/clone) or [Clone a workflow](/api-reference/workflows/clone) (MCP tools: `clone_skill`, `clone_workflow`), then run the copy. A run of a public skill you have not cloned returns `403` `clone_required`.

## Clone a skill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a skill">
    Browse or search the marketplace until you find a skill that matches what you need. Click the card to open the skill's page and review its inputs, sample output, and rating before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Clone skill">
    On the skill's page, click **Clone skill** in the top-right corner. A confirmation dialog will appear — click **Clone skill** to confirm. The skill is cloned into your currently active workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your copy">
    After cloning, you are taken directly to the skill in **My skills**. Because the skill was cloned from the marketplace, a **Rate on Marketplace** button appears in the header — use it after running the skill to leave a review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize and run">
    The cloned skill is a full independent copy. You can edit its name, inputs, and instructions with **Edit skill**. Run it to verify it works for your use case, then wire it into a schedule or call it from the API.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Cloned skills run against your own credit balance — not the original author's. Each run deducts credits from your account the same way as your own skills. See [Credits](/concepts/credits).
</Note>

## Rate a skill you cloned

After running a cloned skill, click **Rate on Marketplace** from the skill's page to submit a star rating and written review. Only users who have cloned a skill can rate it — this keeps reviews grounded in real usage. Your rating feeds into the skill's aggregate score on its marketplace listing.

## Share your own skill

Any skill you have built can be shared with the Valendata community by making it public. There are two ways to do this:

- **At publish time** — in the **Publish Skill** modal, set visibility to **Public** before clicking Publish. The skill will appear in the marketplace immediately.
- **After publishing** — open the skill, click **Edit skill**, and toggle visibility from Private to Public. The skill moves to the marketplace as soon as you save.

Public skills are discoverable by all Valendata users. Other users can read them, and run them once they clone them into their own workspace.
