---
title: "Use the marketplace"
description: "Find a ready-made skill, clone it into your workspace, rate it, or share your own."
---

The **marketplace** is a public library of skills and workflows. Instead of building a skill yourself, find one that does what you need and clone it into your workspace. The copy is yours to run and change. Your changes do not affect the original, and changes to the original are not pushed to your copy.

## Browse

Open **Marketplace** from the main sidebar. It has two tabs:

- **Skills**: single skills you can clone, then run.
- **Workflows**: workflows other people have shared.

The **Skills** tab lists public skills you can search and sort by **Trending**, **Most Runs**, or **Recent**. Each card shows the skill's name, description, and creator. Click a card to open the skill's page, which shows its total runs, clones, rating, and credits per run.

<Tip>
  Search by the site name, the kind of data, or the task.
</Tip>

From code or an AI assistant, find a skill by its task with [Find a skill for a task](/api-reference/marketplace/search) or the `search_skills` [MCP tool](/ai-assistants/mcp-tools#marketplace). Find a workflow the same way with `kind=workflow` or the `search_workflows` tool.

Anyone may read a public skill or workflow. You run only your own copy. Clone it first with [Clone a skill](/api-reference/skills/clone) or [Clone a workflow](/api-reference/workflows/clone) (MCP tools: `clone_skill`, `clone_workflow`), then run the copy. A run of a public skill you have not cloned returns `403` `clone_required`.

## Clone a skill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a skill">
    Browse or search the marketplace. Click a card to open the skill's page and check its inputs, sample output, and rating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Clone skill">
    On the skill's page, click **Clone skill** in the top-right corner, then **Clone skill** again to confirm. The copy goes into the workspace you are in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your copy">
    After cloning, you land on the copy in **My skills**. Its header has a **Rate on Marketplace** button. Use it after you run the skill to leave a review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize and run">
    The copy is fully yours. Change its name, inputs, and instructions with **Edit skill**. Run it to check it does what you need, then schedule it or call it from the API.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Runs of a cloned skill use your credits, not the author's, the same as your own skills. See [Credits](/concepts/credits).
</Note>

## Rate a skill you cloned

After you run a cloned skill, click **Rate on Marketplace** on the skill's page to give it stars and a written review. Only people who cloned a skill can rate it, so ratings come from people who used it. Your rating counts toward the skill's score in the marketplace.

## Share your own skill

To share a skill you built, make it public. There are two ways:

- **When you publish it**: in the **Publish Skill** dialog, set visibility to **Public**, then click **Publish**. It shows in the marketplace right away.
- **Later**: open the skill, click **Edit skill**, and change visibility from **Private** to **Public**. It shows in the marketplace when you save.

Anyone on Valendata can find a public skill. Others can read it, and run it after they clone it into their own workspace.
