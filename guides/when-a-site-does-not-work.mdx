Some websites are easy for skills, and some fight back. Before you build a skill for a site, you can check how skills are doing there. When a site does not work for you, you can ask us to fix it, and we email you when it is.

Why a single run failed, and what Valendata repairs on its own, is on [Keeping skills working](/concepts/keeping-skills-working#why-runs-fail). This page is about the site as a whole.

## Proven sites

A site is **proven** when, over the last 30 days:

- at least 3 different people ran skills on it,
- those skills ran at least 10 times, and
- 90% or more of those runs worked.

The list of proven sites is public at [valendata.com/sites](https://www.valendata.com/sites). Each one shows how many skills ran on it, its success rate, and its runs in the last 30 days. From code, call [List proven sites](/api-reference/sites/list-proven). No key is needed for either.

The numbers come only from real runs. A site drops off the list when its runs stop working.

## Check one site

Search the site at [valendata.com/sites](https://www.valendata.com/sites), or call [Get a site](/api-reference/sites/get) with its domain or any address on it. You get one of three answers:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `proven` | Skills keep working here. |
| `failing` | Skills on this site struggle right now. We are on it. |
| `unproven` | Not enough runs yet to say. Create a skill for it, and once it keeps working for people it joins the list. See [Create a skill](/guides/create-a-skill). |

Counts are hidden unless at least 3 people use the site. With fewer, the numbers could point to one person's runs, so the answer has the status and leaves the counts `null`.

## Ask us to make a site work

When a run or a new skill fails on a site, choose **Make this site work** in the app. From code, call [Create a site request](/api-reference/sites/create-request).

- **One vote per person per site.** Asking again for the same site does not add a second vote. If someone already asked, your vote joins theirs, and `votes` shows how many people want it.
- **You don't have to describe the error.** Pass the skill, run, or creation that failed, and we attach its failure details ourselves. Only your own are read.
- **You get an email when it is fixed.** Everyone who asked for that site is told.

See your requests and whether each is fixed with [List your site requests](/api-reference/sites/list-requests). Changed your mind? [Remove your vote](/api-reference/sites/delete-vote).

## From an AI assistant

Two MCP tools do the same: `site_status` checks a site, and `request_site` asks us to make it work. See [MCP tools](/ai-assistants/mcp-tools#sites).
