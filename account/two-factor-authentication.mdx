## Supported authenticator apps

Any authenticator app that supports TOTP works. For example:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Google Authenticator" icon="google">
    Free, on iOS and Android.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Authy" icon="shield">
    Syncs across devices, with encrypted backups.
  </Card>

  <Card title="1Password" icon="lock">
    A password manager with TOTP codes built in.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Any TOTP app" icon="key">
    Any app that supports TOTP (RFC 6238).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Enable two-factor authentication

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account settings">
    Go to [app.valendata.com](https://app.valendata.com) and open **Settings → Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the 2FA setup">
    Click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**. Valendata shows a QR code for your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open your authenticator app, add an account, and scan the QR code. The app starts showing a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a verification code">
    Enter the current code from your authenticator app and click **Verify**. This checks that the app is set up right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in with 2FA enabled

After your email and password, Valendata asks for your 6-digit code. Open your authenticator app and enter the current code. Each code lasts 30 seconds. If it runs out before you submit, use the next one.

## Disable two-factor authentication

You can turn off 2FA at any time in the same place.

1. Go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Under the **Two-Factor Authentication** section, click **Disable**.
3. Enter the current code from your authenticator app to confirm it is you.
4. 2FA is turned off at once.

<Note>
  Without 2FA, only your password protects your account. If you turn it off for a moment, for example to switch apps, turn it back on soon.
</Note>

## Switching authenticator apps

To move to a new authenticator app or device, turn off 2FA with your current app, then turn it on again and scan the new QR code with the new app.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Does 2FA apply to API key usage?">
  No. API keys sign requests in directly, without 2FA. 2FA applies only when you sign in to the web app. Keep your keys safe and rotate them. See [API keys](/account/api-keys).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see which authenticator app is linked to my account?">
  No. Valendata stores only the TOTP secret, not which app you use. To change the secret or pair a new app, turn 2FA off and on again.
</Accordion>

<Info>
  Two-factor authentication arrived in Valendata **v1.0.1** (2026-06-25).
</Info>
