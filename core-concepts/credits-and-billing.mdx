Valendata uses a transparent, credit-based metering system. You only pay for what your automations consume.

## Cost Breakdown

Every execution cost is calculated from three components:

1. **LLM Tokens**: The model reasoning and vision analysis performed during the task.
2. **Browser Execution Time**: The duration (in seconds) the Chromium session is active.
3. **External Proxies / Captcha Solves**: High-tier residential proxy usage or automated challenge solving.

```text
Total Cost = (LLM Input + Output Tokens) + (Browser Seconds × Rate) + Add-ons
```

## Auto-Recharge

To avoid task interruptions on high-volume schedules:

- **Threshold**: Set a balance trigger (e.g. 500 credits).
- **Top-up Amount**: Automatically charges your default card for your preferred bundle (e.g. 2,500 credits) whenever the balance drops below the threshold.
