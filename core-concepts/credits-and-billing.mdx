## What's Included in Every Plan

Every Valendata plan gives you unconstrained access to:

- 🌐 **Remote Cloud Browsers**: Isolated high-performance Chromium instances with live viewport streaming & Human-in-the-loop (HITL) handoff.
- 🛡️ **Stealth Proxies**: Automatic residential & datacenter IP rotation to prevent bot bans and IP rate limits.
- 🧩 **Automated CAPTCHA Solver**: Automatic resolution of Cloudflare Turnstile, reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, and challenge interstitials.
- 📬 **Artifacts Inbox**: Direct extraction downloads (JSON, CSV, Markdown, Screenshots, and MP4 screen recordings).
- 🪄 **Skills & Parameterized Prompts**: Save natural language automations with reusable `{{variable}}` fields.
- ⚡ **Multi-Step Workflows**: Visually build and execute complex chained automation pipelines.
- ⏰ **Scheduled Automations**: Run tasks on cron schedules or custom periodic intervals.
- 🔌 **Connectors & Integrations**: Sync outputs directly into Google Drive, Google Sheets, Slack, Webhooks, or MCP.
- 🔑 **Bring Your Own Keys (BYOK)**: Use Valendata's default AI models or plug in your personal OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini API keys.

## Flexible Top-ups

Need more credits mid-billing cycle? Paid subscribers can top up any custom amount of credits on demand ($1 = 100 credits) directly from the **Buy Credits** console.

## Auto-Recharge

To avoid task interruptions on high-volume schedules:

- **Threshold**: Set a balance trigger (e.g. 500 credits).
- **Top-up Amount**: Automatically charges your default payment method for your preferred bundle (e.g. 2,000 credits) whenever the balance drops below the threshold.
