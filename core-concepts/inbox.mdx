---
title: "Managed Verification Inbox"
description: "Automatic email verification and OTP code extraction mid-session."
---


Automating workflows like user sign-ups, job applications, or portal registrations frequently requires confirming a verification email or entering a 6-digit OTP code.

## How it Works

1. Valendata assigns a unique virtual inbox address to your workspace session (`runner-921@inbox.valendata.app`).
2. When the automation fills out a form, it enters this email address.
3. The email arrives in real-time — Valendata's background mail worker parses the message, extracts the OTP or confirmation link, and delivers it directly to the active browser agent.
4. The agent types the code into the form and completes the workflow uninterrupted.
