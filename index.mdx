## How Valendata Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe or record a task">
    Write a plain-English prompt like "extract every product from the bestsellers page". Valendata's AI plans every step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish as a Skill">
    One click turns your task into a versioned, callable API endpoint. You get a live URL and an API key — no infrastructure required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule or integrate">
    Call your Skill from any codebase, wire it into a Workflow, or schedule it on a cron. Fresh data lands wherever you need it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
